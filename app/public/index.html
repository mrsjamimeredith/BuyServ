Connect Pinterest

One-time setup needed

Before connecting, add your Pinterest app credentials:

  1. Create a Pinterest app at developers.pinterest.com/apps.
  2. Set the redirect URI to http://localhost:3000/auth/callback.
  3. Copy .env.example to .env and fill in PINTEREST_CLIENT_ID + PINTEREST_CLIENT_SECRET.
  4. Restart the app.

  • One-click pins — title, image, affiliate link, board. Done.
  • Bulk CSV upload — import hundreds of products, post them all with one button.
  • Saved drafts — review before posting, retry failures, never lose a product.
